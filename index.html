How to Use?

1

You can edit the amount of credits for a course. If your thesis is worth 4 credits, then edit the credit field of that course and change it to 4.

2

The CGPA gets rounded up if the value of the third decimal digit is 5 or more. For example, if you got 3.895, then the system will show it as 3.90.
