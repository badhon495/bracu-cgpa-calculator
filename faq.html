How to Use the BRACU CGPA Calculator?

1

Enter Your Current CGPA: Input your current CGPA from BRAC University. This BRACU CG calculator supports the standard 4.0 grading scale used by BRACU.

2

Enter Total Credits Completed: Enter the total number of credit hours you have completed before this semester. This is used to weight your existing CGPA correctly.

3

Add Course Credits: You can edit the credits for each course. If your thesis or project is worth 4 credits, simply change the credit field to 4. This BRACU calculator handles variable credit courses.

4

Select Your Grades: Choose your grade for each course from the dropdown. Grades follow BRACU's standard scale: A (4.0), A- (3.7), B+ (3.3), B (3.0), and so on down to F (0.0).

5

BRACU Grading System: The CGPA gets rounded up if the third decimal digit is 5 or more. For example, 3.895 becomes 3.90 according to BRAC University's grading system.

6

Important Note for Retakes & Repeats: If you have multiple retake or repeat courses, this simple CGPA calculator may not provide an accurate result due to BRAC University's complex grading policies for repeated courses. For precise CGPA calculation, please use our Extended CGPA Calculator designed specifically for handling retakes and repeats.

7

Contribute to BRACU Community: Help improve this BRACU CGPA calculator! Visit our GitHub repository to contribute and suggest improvements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1

How do I calculate my CGPA at BRAC University?
Multiply each course's credit hours by its grade point value, sum all grade points, then divide by total credits. BRACU rounds up if the third decimal is 5 or more. This calculator does it automatically.

Q2

What grading scale does BRAC University use?
BRACU uses a 4.0 scale: A=4.0, A-=3.7, B+=3.3, B=3.0, B-=2.7, C+=2.3, C=2.0, C-=1.7, D+=1.3, D=1.0, D-=0.7, F=0.0.

Q3

How does BRACU round the final CGPA?
BRAC University rounds CGPA up when the third decimal digit is 5 or higher. For example, 3.895 becomes 3.90, while 3.894 stays 3.89.

Q4

Can I use this for retake or repeat courses?
This simple calculator may not be accurate for retakes due to BRACU's complex repeat-course policies. Use the Extended CGPA Calculator for retake scenarios.

Q5

Is this BRACU CGPA Calculator free?
Yes — completely free, no login required, and no data is stored or shared.

Q6

What is the maximum CGPA at BRAC University?
The maximum CGPA is 4.0, achieved by earning an A in every course.

Q7

How many credits can I take per semester at BRACU?
Most BRACU courses are 3 credit hours. Thesis or project courses may be 4 or more. This calculator supports variable credit hours — just edit the credit field per course.
